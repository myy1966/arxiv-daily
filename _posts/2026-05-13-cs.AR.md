## 新投稿 (3)

<h3 id="1">[1] Sieve: Dynamic Expert-Aware PIM Acceleration for Evolving Mixture-of-Experts Models</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2605.11277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11277)
- **Authors**: Jungwoo Kim, Rubens Lacouture, Genghan Zhang, Gina Sohn, Qizheng Zhang, Swapnil Gandhi, Christos Kozyrakis, Kunle Olukotun
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Mixture-of-Experts, LLM Inference, Compute-in-Memory
- **Summary**: 该论文针对现代MoE模型中专家分布的双峰特性，提出了一种名为Sieve的运行时框架，通过动态调度器将专家执行在GPU和PIM之间进行分区，有效提升了MoE推理的吞吐量和交互性。

<h3 id="2">[2] Heterogeneous SoC Integrating an Open-Source Recurrent SNN Accelerator for Neuromorphic Edge Computing on FPGA</h3>

- **Authors**: Michelangelo Barocci, Vittorio Fra, Enrico Macii, Gianvito Urgese
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI
- **Tags**: Neuromorphic Computing, FPGA, Edge Computing
- **Venue**: ECML-PKDD 2024 Workshop
- **Summary**: 该论文提出了一种异构SoC设计，将开源循环SNN加速器ReckOn与传统处理器集成在FPGA上，实现了神经形态边缘计算平台，并验证了其分类精度和在线学习能力。

<h3 id="3">[3] Enhancing Instruction Prefetching via Cache and TLB Management</h3>

- **Authors**: Alexandre Valentin Jamet, Georgios Vavouliotis, Marti Torrents, Dimitrios Chasapis, Marc Casas
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.PF
- **Tags**: Hardware Architecture, Instruction Prefetching
- **Venue**: ISCA 2026

- **Authors**: Xiaolong Deng
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.ET
