<h3 id="1">[1] FP8 is All You Need (Part 1): Debunking Hardware FP64 as the HPC Holy Grail</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.06510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06510)
- **Authors**: Satoshi Matsuoka
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI; cs.DC; cs.PF
- **Tags**: FP8 Computing, High Performance Computing
- **Summary**: 传统HPC依赖硬件FP64，但AI加速器上FP64性能严重下降。提出使用FP8结合Ozaki Scheme II和Kulisch方法模拟FP64精度。在B300上模拟FP64达到约500 TFLOPS，远超原生FP64，验证了FP8即可满足生产HPC需求。

<h3 id="2">[2] DxPTA: An Architecture Design Space Exploration with Optical Dataflow-guided Strategy for HW/SW Co-Design of Photonic Transformer Accelerators</h3>

- **Authors**: Rachmad Vidya Wicaksana Putra, Solomon Micheal Serunjogi, Mahmoud Rasras, Muhammad Shafique
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI; cs.DC; cs.ET; cs.LG
- **Tags**: Photonic Computing, Design Space Exploration
- **Summary**: 现有光子Transformer加速器设计未考虑应用约束且手动设计耗时。提出DxPTA设计空间探索方法，基于相干光数据流参数分析实现约束感知架构搜索。搜索速度比穷举法快15.2倍，满足给定约束。

<h3 id="3">[3] P-Cast Precision in FP8 Attention: Sink-Induced Collapse and the Optimality of S=2^8</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.06521](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06521)
- **Authors**: Reed Lau
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI; cs.DC; cs.LG; cs.PF
- **Tags**: FP8 Computing, Attention Mechanism
- **Summary**: FP8注意力计算中P矩阵转换导致精度损失。分析发现反向KV迭代和S=256静态缩放可消除P-collapse。实现3-10倍MSE改进，给出精度损失阈值公式。
