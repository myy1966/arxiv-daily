## 新投稿 (6)

<h3 id="1">[1] BIDENT: Heterogeneous Operator-level Mapping for Efficient Edge Inference</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.05271](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05271)
- **Authors**: Hoseok Kim, Arghadip Das, Soumendu Ghosh, Arnab Raha, Vijay Raghunathan
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Heterogeneous Computing, Edge Computing, DNN Deployment
- **Summary**: 针对当前边缘推理将整个模型映射到单一处理单元导致性能与能效不足的问题，提出BIDENT框架，在算子级别将不同算子分配到最合适的处理单元，通过最短路径问题实现最优调度。实验表明，在Intel Core Ultra SoC上，该框架在并发场景下实现最高3.42倍加速和平均48.2%的能耗降低。

<h3 id="2">[2] MOSAIC: A Workload-Driven Simulation and Design-Space Exploration Framework for Heterogeneous NPUs</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.05362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05362)
- **Authors**: Arghadip Das, Hoseok Kim, Soomin Lee, Arnab Raha, Deepak A Mathaikutty, Vijay Raghunathan
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Hardware Acceleration, Heterogeneous Computing, Design Space Exploration
- **Summary**: 针对新兴AI架构（如状态空间模型、KAN等）非MAC密集型计算导致同质NPU效率低下的问题，提出MOSAIC框架，支持细粒度异构NPU微架构设计与空间探索。在20个工作负载上，最佳通用HPU相比同质基线实现平均46.91%的等面积能耗节省。

<h3 id="3">[3] FQA: A Full-Space Quantization-Driven Architecture for Hardware-Efficient Piecewise Approximation of Nonlinear Activation Functions</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.05627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05627)
- **Authors**: Chenjun Hao, Feng Yan, Hongbing Pan, Yuxuan Wang
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.ET
- **Tags**: Quantization, Hardware Acceleration
- **Summary**: 针对硬件分段多项式近似中量化误差导致最优系数偏移的问题，提出全空间量化驱动架构FQA，综合考虑小数截断与量化误差，并设计TBW加速方法。实验表明，Sigmoid函数硬件实现面积和功耗降低超过50%。

<h3 id="4">[4] Space-CIM: Enabling Compute-In-Memory Accelerators for Thermally-Constrained Space Platforms</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.05741](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05741)
- **Authors**: Sohan Salahuddin Mugdho, Md. Shahedul Hasan, Cheng Wang
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.ET
- **Tags**: Compute-in-Memory, Energy Efficiency, Space Computing
- **Venue**: ACM/IEEE International Symposium on Low Power Electronics and Design (ISLPED '26)
- **Summary**: 针对太空数据中心热约束限制计算能力的问题，提出辐射器协同设计方法，比较GPU与存内计算(CIM)加速器。结果表明CIM在太空热约束下具有更均匀的热分布和更高的TOPS/W，优势显著。

<h3 id="5">[5] ITP-STDP: An Intrinsic-Timing Power-of-Two Learning Engine for On-Chip SNN Training</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.06159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06159)
- **Authors**: Haihang Xia, Xinyu Zhao, Xuecheng Wang, John Goodenough, Charith Abhayaratne, Panagiotis A. Panagiotou, Chunyi Song, Tiantai Deng
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI; cs.NE
- **Tags**: Spiking Neural Network, Hardware Acceleration, Energy Efficiency
- **Summary**: 针对SNN片上训练中权重更新计算开销大的问题，提出ITP-STDP算法及硬件架构，消除STDP大部分计算开销。在FPGA上能效提升4.5倍至219.8倍，ASIC上速度提升4.8倍至22.01倍，面积仅占1.2%至3.3%。

- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: FPGA, 3D Integration, FPGA Routing
