<h3 id="1">[1] Opto-ViT-v2: Noise-Resilient On-Chip Fine-Tuning for Photonic Near-Sensor Vision Transformer Accelerators</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2607.19421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19421)
- **Authors**: Xuming Chen, Deniz Najafi, Mehrdad Morsali, Chengwei Zhou, Zahra Ghanaatianjobzari, Mahdi Nikdast, Shaahin Angizi, Gourav Datta
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI
- **Tags**: Photonic Computing, On-Device Learning, Hardware Accelerator
- **Venue**: IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD 2026)
- **Summary**: 针对光子视觉Transformer加速器片上微调困难的问题，提出Opto-ViT-v2框架，采用张量低秩分解分离预训练光权重与可训练电子因子，并引入梯度累积稀疏分类器。实验表明，在光子噪声下精度恢复至软件精度的0.3%-0.8%，能效超过100 KFPS/W。
