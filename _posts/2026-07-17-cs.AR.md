<h3 id="1">[1] ExaGEMM: Exploration Framework for CPU-Driven ML Inference via Associative In-Register Computing for Low-Bit GEMM</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2607.14622](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14622)
- **Authors**: Hyunwoo Oh, Suyeon Jang, Hanning Chen, Sanggeon Yun, Ryozo Masukawa, Mohsen Imani
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.LG; cs.OS
- **Tags**: LLM Inference, Quantization, Hardware Acceleration
- **Venue**: ICCAD 2026
- **Summary**: 问题：低比特GEMM在CPU上执行效率低，现有方法难以在碎片化精度和固定SIMD/寄存器预算下权衡。方法：提出ExaGEMM，一种工作负载感知的协同设计框架，通过寄存器驻留LUT执行和轻量级SIMD ISA扩展，利用分析模型剪枝99.2%的候选空间。结果：相比纯软件基线，延迟提升13.29倍，尤其对混合精度LLM工作负载有效。

<h3 id="2">[2] Toward Energy-Efficient and Low-Power Arrhythmia Detection for Wearable Devices</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2607.14747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14747)
- **Authors**: Floriaan Bulten, Yawar Rasheed, Arlene John, Vincenzo Stoico, Ghayoor Gillani
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.LG; cs.NE; cs.PF
- **Tags**: Medical AI, Energy Efficiency, Approximate Computing
- **Summary**: 问题：深度学习用于可穿戴设备心律失常检测时功耗高。方法：采用数据精度降低和近似乘法等近似技术，优化深度学习模型及其硬件架构。结果：在MIT-BIH数据库上，功耗降低64.9%（3.07 μW），分类准确率93.7%，灵敏度92.1%；100 MHz时能耗降低61.5%（0.8 μJ）。

- **Venue**: MICRO 2026
