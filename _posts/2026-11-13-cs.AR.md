<h3 id="1">[1] SlipStream: Enabling HBF for GPU Systems by Mitigating the Latency and SRAM Overheads</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2611.14776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14776)
- **Authors**: Anish Saxena, Hritvik Taneja, Christos Kozyrakis, Aamer Jaleel
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.DC; cs.LG
- **Tags**: LLM Inference, Hardware-Software Co-design
- **Venue**: IEEE International Symposium on High-Performance Computer Architecture (HPCA) 2027
- **Summary**: LLM推理受限于内存容量，HBF虽能提供16倍容量但读延迟高达约20μs，按需访问会使推理慢12.8倍。本文提出SlipStream软硬件协同预取器，将预取转化为由需求节奏驱动的生产者-消费者流水线，以4KB页为粒度跟踪并仅在页返回时分配缓冲。在七个16-800GB稠密模型上，40MB缓冲达到理想HBF性能的92.4%，而H3仅43.1%；16MB缓冲版本性能差距在4%以内，并支持MoE模型达到91.2%。

<h3 id="19">[19] HAWK: Heterogeneity-Aware KV Cache Transfer for Efficient GPU-NPU Phase-Disaggregated LLM Inference</h3>

- **Authors**: Junhyeok Park, Jungeun Yoon, Kyungmin Kim, Hunsoon Lee, Shinyoung Ahn, Kangho Kim
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.DC
