<h3 id="1">[1] Optimizing Polynomial Multiplication and Fixed-Weight Sampling for HQC on ARM Cortex-M4</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2608.18145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18145)
- **Authors**: Jihoon Jang, Hanbeom Shin, Suhri Kim, Seokhie Hong, Donggeun Kwon
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Post-Quantum Cryptography, Optimization
- **Summary**: 针对HQC在ARM Cortex-M4上的实现效率问题，提出面向多项式乘法与固定权重采样的优化方法，包括脏感知寄存器分配、XOR重排序、更稀疏的FAFFT模数及预执行展开。在NUCLEO-L4R5ZI上，密钥生成、封装和解封装分别比现有最优实现快33.1%、34.6%和29.8%。

<h3 id="2">[2] TokenPowerSandbox: Evidence-Gated CPU-First Screening for Energy-Aware LLM Serving</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2608.18149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18149)
- **Authors**: Chenxu Niu
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI; cs.DC; cs.LG; cs.PF
- **Tags**: LLM Serving, Energy Efficiency
- **Summary**: 针对能耗感知LLM服务中配置评估昂贵且预测器可能过度自信的问题，提出基于证据门控的CPU优先筛查流程TokenPowerSandbox，结合可解释CPU预测器、目标GPU短探针、全负载验证及防篡改溯源。在H100上对Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct进行51次冻结后运行，能量MAPE为6.23%/7.35%，Spearman秩相关0.976/0.933，且TTFT门控在并发数低于4时触发弃权。

<h3 id="3">[3] A Fault-Tolerant Spike-Time Interface for Approximate Agreement in Distributed Neuromorphic Systems</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2608.18151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18151)
- **Authors**: Arman Ferdowsi, Maryam DehghanChenary, Kevin Tierney, Atakan Aral
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.IT
- **Tags**: Neuromorphic Computing, Fault Tolerance, Distributed Computing
- **Summary**: 针对分布式神经形态系统中共享控制参数副本因拜占庭故障而发散的问题，提出Spike-time Interface for Faults（SIF），结合定步长时段、发送方归属、首脉冲准入、有界定时误差与静默哨兵，并采用SpikeTrim的MSR规则。当n≥3f+1时保证单步鲁棒有效性和紧致无噪声收缩因子f/(n-2f)，仿真展示了容错阈值与恢复能力。

<h3 id="5">[5] CTTE: An Open Dual-Protocol RISC-V Trace Encoder for N-Trace and E-Trace</h3>

- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: RISC-V, Hardware Design, Debugging
