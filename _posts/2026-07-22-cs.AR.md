<h3 id="1">[1] A Progressive Approach to Synthesizable RTL Design Generation Using LLMs</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2607.18519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18519)
- **Authors**: Xiangfei Kong, Tasnim Tabassum, Marwan Abdelwahab, Hao Zheng
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: RTL Generation, LLM Reasoning
- **Summary**: 论文针对LLM生成RTL设计时理解错误频发且缺乏对意图解释验证的问题，提出VeriRefine方法。该方法通过逐步精炼规范说明为抽象信号转换函数（ASTF），经过五层审计确保解释正确性，再将仿真失败分类为理解或编码错误进行定向修复。在Claude Sonnet 4.6上，VeriRefine在RTLLM v2.0和VerilogEval-Human v2上分别达到94.0%和98.1%的功能正确性。

<h3 id="2">[2] PIP-NTT: Towards a Scalable Memory-Parallelized Accelerator for Iterative NTT in PQC</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2607.18533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18533)
- **Authors**: Malik Imran, Ayesha Khalid, Ciara Rafferty, Safiullah Khan, Muhammad Rashid, Maire O&#39;Neill
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.CR
- **Tags**: Hardware Accelerator, Post-Quantum Cryptography
- **Venue**: IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computing (TETC)
- **Summary**: 针对迭代NTT加速器中乒乓存储和大存储块限制效率的问题，提出PIP-NTT。采用四个n/4大小存储器的并行化策略和乘法无缩放架构，并对Cooley-Tukey与Gentleman-Sande蝶形单元进行设计空间探索。在FPGA上实现比现有最优面积优化和高速设计分别提升2.67倍和1.48倍的平均面积-时间积效率。

<h3 id="3">[3] An Efficient Fault-Tolerance Scheme for CKKS Computation on CPUs</h3>
