## 新投稿 (4)

<h3 id="1">[1] SPINE: A Fault Injection Profiler for Quantized Neural Networks under Accumulated Faults</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.19526](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19526)
- **Authors**: Nathan Guimarães, Ian Kersz, Leonardo R. Gobatto, Fabio Benevenuti, Michael G. Jordan, Antonio Carlos S. Beck, Fernanda L. Kastensmidt, Jose Rodrigo Azambuja
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Fault Tolerance, Quantization, Edge Computing
- **Summary**: 针对量化神经网络在边缘部署中辐射诱导比特翻转的故障累积问题，提出基于GDB的权值位翻转注入框架，无需重训练或代码修改即可生成逐层故障概要。实验表明不同拓扑和量化方案下选择性加固策略的有效性。

<h3 id="2">[2] A Tool for the Synthesis of Adaptive Probabilistic Processors Based on the Ising Model</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.19533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19533)
- **Authors**: Jonathan Juracy Carneiro da Silva, Leonardo R. Gobatto, Jose Rodrigo Azambuja
- **Subjects**: cs.AR; cs.AI
- **Tags**: Ising Machine, Combinatorial Optimization
- **Summary**: 针对组合优化问题映射到伊辛模型的概率处理器综合与模拟需求，提出自动构建伊辛哈密顿量并自适应选择更新算法的工具。实验显示比固定方法更好的收敛性和灵活性。

<h3 id="3">[3] Design and Evaluation of Energy-Efficient Whisper Dot-Product Kernel Offloading on a CGLA Architecture</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.19913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19913)
- **Authors**: Takuto Ando, Yu Eto, Ayumu Takeuchi, Yasuhiko Nakashima
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: CGRA, Speech Processing, Energy Efficiency
- **Venue**: Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience
- **Summary**: 针对Whisper点积运算占用大部分执行时间的问题，在CGLA架构IMAX上实现点积核卸载，通过本地内存分配和突发调度优化。FPGA原型和28nm ASIC投影表明在tiny模型下能效远低于Jetson和RTX。

<h3 id="4">[4] ExSpike: A General Full-Event Neuromorphic Architecture for Exploiting Irregular Sparsity with Event Compression</h3>
- **arXiv**: [2606.20414](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20414)
- **Authors**: Yuehai Chen, Farhad Merchant
- **Subjects**: cs.AR
- **Tags**: Spiking Neural Network, Neuromorphic Computing, Hardware Acceleration
- **Venue**: 36th International Conference on Field-Programmable Logic and Applications (FPL 2026)
- **Code**: [code](https://github.com/xiaoyuehai/ExSpike)
- **Summary**: 针对脉冲神经网络不规则稀疏性难以转化为性能增益的问题，提出全事件神经形态架构ExSpike，通过纯事件驱动数据流和相邻位置事件压缩提高计算效率。FPGA实现达到高能效，优于当前最优。

- **Tags**: RISC-V, Hardware Acceleration, Edge Computing, Tsetlin Machine
- **Venue**: IEEE ISVLSI Conference 2026
- **Summary**: 针对Tsetlin Machine加速器依赖外部主机限制灵活性的问题，设计面向TM推理的简化RISC-V处理器，通过指令精简和路径优化降低能耗。实验表明相比二值神经网络，精度更高且能耗降低29.7倍。
